What is this?

A Manifest V3 browser extension that goes beyond cookie banner clicking to provide a comprehensive, extensible data permission layer.

Built on

  • Consent-O-Matic (MIT) — CMP detection & rule engine
  • Chrome Declarative Net Request — GPC header injection
  • Manifest V3 — modern extension architecture

Legal

Crumb Control is released under the MIT licence and is provided as-is, with no warranty.

It stores your settings on your own device and sends no data anywhere. It is a convenience tool, not legal advice, and it does not guarantee that any given website honours the choices it submits.

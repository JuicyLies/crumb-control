What is this?

Crumb Control answers cookie banners for you. You set your preferences once and it picks the right buttons on every site you visit — so you stop clicking "Reject All" through three layers of menus.

It's the browser-level consent setting the EU's ePrivacy Regulation was supposed to give everyone, before lobbying shelved it.

How it works

  • 209 CMP rules — recognises OneTrust, Cookiebot, Quantcast, Didomi, Sourcepoint, Iubenda, Usercentrics and 200+ more
  • Accent-tolerant matching — banners in Italian, French, German, Spanish and other languages match even with accents, curly apostrophes or odd spacing
  • Global Privacy Control — sends a Sec-GPC: 1 header, which some laws legally require sites to honour
  • Per-site overrides — tap any decision on the Status tab to change it for that site alone

Your privacy

No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no servers. Your settings, your activity log and your banner counter never leave this browser.

The only network request it makes is the Sec-GPC header on sites you're already visiting.

Honest limitations

  • Some banners still aren't recognised. When Crumb Control isn't sure, it leaves the banner alone rather than clicking blind — that's deliberate.
  • It never fakes consent. If you say reject, it clicks reject.
  • Sending a signal doesn't force a site to obey it, especially outside the EU and California.
  • It won't bypass paywalls, and it doesn't touch strictly-necessary cookies.

Hit a banner it missed? Use Report broken cookie banner on the Status tab — that's how coverage improves.

Built on

  • Consent-O-Matic (Aarhus University, MIT) — CMP detection & rule engine
  • Declarative Net Request — GPC header injection
  • Manifest V3 — modern extension architecture
